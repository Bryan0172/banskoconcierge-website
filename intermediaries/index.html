Your property stays in your hands. We review before anything circulates.

For firms that do not advertise: off-market advisers, family offices, co-brokers, notaries, surveyors, estate and insolvency administrators, hotel brokers with quiet mandates. If you move a property quietly, you do not need another agent. You need a counterpart who can review the building before documents start to circulate.

How we work confidentially

In a quiet sale the concern is rarely finding no buyer. It is the property doing the rounds and losing value. Our process is built around that:

01On a search mandate we represent the buyer only.
02We share confidential property details only with the recipients agreed in writing beforehand.
03No listing on our website, no property on social media, no mailing list.
04We agree your role and the fee arrangements for our cooperation in writing before work begins.

What we bring: the review

An intermediary knows the owner and the market. What is often missing is a sound assessment of the building before a buyer asks for it. That is our part.

  • Engineer-led review on site: condition, construction, damp, building services and realistic renovation cost, from our own construction experience in the region.
  • We interpret official registry statistics on transaction activity and distinguish them from asking prices.
  • Legal and tax questions are handled by suitably qualified local professionals; we coordinate, we do not replace them.

The documented assessment provides a basis for viewings and negotiations.

Our role, in writing

For every property we disclose whom we act for. If we were to be paid by both sides in a transaction, that is a conflict of interest. We name it beforehand and proceed only with the written consent of everyone involved. Where a transaction requires a regulated brokerage role, it sits with the licensed firm that holds the mandate.

The seller does not determine the technical findings. Before proceeding with an introduction, we establish permission to share all material findings. If a material finding is to be withheld, we do not proceed with the introduction.
