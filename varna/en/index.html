Prime location: where we work

Varna is where we started. Our work does not end there.

Varna

Where we started

The sea capital of Bulgaria: port city, Sea Garden and one of the country's most important archaeological museums, home to one of the oldest gold treasures in the world. In the resort of St. Constantine and Helena, the oldest seaside resort on the Bulgarian coast, seven thermal springs (40 to 60 °C) rise alongside beaches and promenade. Varna Airport.

Burgas

Black Sea coast, south

The port city at the southern end of the coast opens the way to two historic old towns of rank: Nessebar (UNESCO World Heritage) and Sozopol. Nature lovers find lakes and wetlands outside the city and, to the south, Strandzha Nature Park, the largest protected area in Bulgaria. Own airport.

Sofia

The capital

Business, administration and culture, with the Boyana Church (UNESCO World Heritage) and the Vitosha mountains at the city's doorstep: skiing in winter, hiking in summer. The country's main international airport.

Bansko

Pirin mountains

At the foot of Pirin National Park (UNESCO World Heritage): skiing straight from town in winter, trails to the mountain lakes below Vihren (2,914 m) in summer. A historic old town, lived in year-round, home base of Bansko Concierge.

Further regions on request. Direct flights from Varna, selection, partly seasonal, as of October 2026: Frankfurt, Munich, Vienna, Zurich, Brussels, London, Milan, Rome, Berlin, Tel Aviv, Istanbul. Schedules change, please check before booking.

Investor guide: your path in six steps

A structured process, the way it is expected internationally. Every step has a defined result.

  1. Mandate and strategyObjectives, horizon, capital framework and risk appetite are set down in a written investment brief.
  2. Market and location screeningRegions and micro-locations are compared against your brief: demand drivers, access, regulatory context.
  3. Shortlist and accessCandidates from our network, also from our cooperation with ATLANTA, and where relevant off-market. Our technical review is independent of the seller. Each one is tested against your brief.
  4. Due diligenceTechnical review of building fabric, permits and as-built condition, plus legal title and encumbrances. Legal and tax parts are carried out by independent specialists we coordinate.
  5. Structure and transactionAcquisition structure and tax set-up with independent advisers, negotiation support, notarial deed and land registry entry.
  6. Handover and asset careHandover and snagging, renovation or fit-out coordination, ongoing care and reporting.

The investor guide describes our process. It is not investment, legal or tax advice and contains no return forecasts.
